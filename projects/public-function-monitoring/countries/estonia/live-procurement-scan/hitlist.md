## 🔴 IMMEDIATE PRIORITY (Action within 2 weeks)

### 1. RIT Microsoft License Framework
**Status:** Pre-tender phase
**Expected publication:** June 2026 (first half)
**Estimated value:** €10M+ (36-month framework)

**Why it matters:**
- Locks all state agencies into Microsoft for 3 years
- No competitive evaluation of alternatives
- Open-source solutions (LibreOffice, Nextcloud) ignored

**Intervention:**
- File pre-tender objection citing Article 38 (neutral specifications)
- Demand market consultation including open-source vendors
- Request inclusion of "or equivalent" language

**Contact:**
- RIT: info@rit.ee
- Rahandusministeerium Public Procurement Policy

**Documents needed:**
- Technical specification review
- Cost comparison with open-source alternatives

## 🟠 HIGH PRIORITY (Action within 1 month)

### 3. VMware/Broadcom Migration Requirements
**Deadline:** March 2026 (Broadcom ultimatum)
**Affected:** Estonian cloud providers, government systems

**Why it matters:**
- WaveCom (Estonian company) invested 15 years in VMware
- Forced migration or 400%+ cost increase
- Government systems potentially affected

**Intervention:**
- Support CISPE EU legal challenge
- Monitor Estonian government VMware dependencies
- Advocate for migration assistance fund

**Related entities:**
- RIA (Information System Authority)
- KEMIT (Ministry IT Center)
- WaveCom OÜ

## 📝 HOW TO FILE A COMPLAINT (Vaidlustus)

### Riigihangete Vaidlustuskomisjon
**Address:** Endla 13, 10122 Tallinn
**Deadline:** 10 days from learning of violation
**Fee:** €640 - €3,200 (based on contract value)

### Required content:
1. Hankija nimi (Contracting authority name)
2. Hanke viitenumber (Tender reference number)
3. Rikkumise kirjeldus (Description of violation)
4. Nõue (Remedy requested)
5. Tõendid (Evidence)

### Online filing:
Via Riigihangete register (riigihanked.riik.ee)
