# Vision Paper Analysis Rubric

## Context

We're evaluating whether the 12-layer Agentic State framework fits a **public interest framing** — where the core question is:

> "In an era of many AGIs, how do we ensure they serve the public interest?"

The audience is **democratic political decision-makers** (parliament, PM, ministers) — where politics mediates civil society interests into state action.

## The Reframe

- **Old frame:** "Governments should adopt agentic AI" (government capacity as goal)
- **New frame:** "Will the public interest be served in the AGI era?" (public interest as goal, government capacity as instrument)

## Analysis Questions for Each Layer

### 1. Current Framing
- What is the chapter actually arguing?
- Who is the implicit subject? (Government? Citizens? Public interest?)
- What problem is being solved?

### 2. Public Interest Alignment
- Does this layer explicitly connect to public interest outcomes?
- Or is it framed primarily around government efficiency/capability?
- Are citizens/public positioned as beneficiaries or as passive recipients?

### 3. Accountability & Democratic Oversight
- Does the layer address how democratic oversight works?
- Are there mechanisms for public input, contestation, redress?
- Does it assume government serves public interest, or does it create mechanisms to ensure it?

### 4. Outside-In Pressures
- Does the layer acknowledge external pressures (inbound agents, civil society, adversarial actors)?
- Or is it purely inside-out (what government should build)?

### 5. Multi-Vector Theory of Change
- Does the layer assume only government builds/does this?
- Or does it acknowledge civil society, permissionless innovation, external pressure as valid vectors?

### 6. Gap Assessment
- **Green:** Layer already fits public interest framing well
- **Yellow:** Layer is compatible but needs reframing/additions
- **Red:** Layer has fundamental tensions with public interest framing

### 7. Specific Recommendations
- What would need to change to strengthen public interest framing?
- Are these changes additive (new sections) or substantive (rewrites)?
